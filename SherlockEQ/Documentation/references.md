# References

SherlockEQ's documentation cites credible sources where it discusses hearing,
tinnitus, audiograms, psychoacoustics, headphone correction, metering, or
digital signal processing. This page consolidates them. Each topic article also
lists the specific references it relies on.

SherlockEQ is **not a medical device** and these references do not constitute
medical advice. They are provided so claims can be checked against their
sources.
